**Problems:**

- The hook is weak. The 'Model Failed' message is a common, somewhat cliché error. It needs a more immediate, surprising element to grab attention. The zoom is standard but doesn’t add much.
- While generally understandable, the script relies heavily on jargon ('model meltdown', 'training data'). A broader audience won’t immediately grasp the core issue – poor data quality.
- The length is acceptable, but could be tightened. The voiceover in Scene 2 is particularly wordy.
- The tone is generally correct – slightly exasperated – but leans a little too heavily into the ‘engineer pointing at spreadsheet’ trope. It feels slightly staged.
- The hashtag selection is decent, but could be more targeted. ‘Chaos’ feels a bit out of place.

**Fixes suggested:**

- Replace the opening error message with something more visually arresting and unexpected. Consider a short, looping animation of data corruption or a distorted model visualization. Aim for immediate intrigue.
- Simplify the voiceover in Scene 2. Instead of describing the messy data, focus on the *impact* of that mess: ‘Our model was trained on inconsistent dates, leading to unpredictable results.’
- Trim the voiceover in Scene 2 by approximately 20-30%. Focus on the core problem – bad data – and its consequence.
- Add a brief, relatable visual element to Scene 3. Instead of just pointing, show the engineer visibly frustrated while reviewing the data. A quick cut to a stressed expression would improve the impact.
- Replace ‘Chaos’ with a more specific hashtag like #DataErrors or #TrainingDataIssues. Consider adding #DataGovernance or #DataQualityControl.
