**Problems:**

- The hook is too vague. ‘Ever wonder what’s hiding in plain sight?’ is a common, uninspired phrase. It doesn’t immediately convey the surprising element of finding a face. The voiceover in Scene 1 feels rushed and doesn’t build anticipation effectively. The ‘You won’t believe what it found!’ is a cliché and doesn’t deliver on the initial intrigue.
- The script relies heavily on technical jargon ('algorithm,' 'patterns') which will be confusing to a general audience. While ‘computer vision’ is a relevant hashtag, the script doesn't explain what it *is* in a way that’s accessible.
- The ‘Explore Computer Vision!’ call to action feels tacked on and doesn’t integrate smoothly with the narrative. It’s a generic sales pitch, not a natural conclusion to the demonstration.

**Fixes suggested:**

- Rewrite the hook to be more specific and intriguing. Example: ‘This AI found a face in a random stock photo – and you won’t believe how.’.  Adjust Scene 1 voiceover to build suspense - perhaps a question like, ‘What if a computer could see something we missed?’
- Simplify the voiceover in Scene 2. Instead of ‘We trained a system to find patterns,’ try ‘We showed the system images and asked it to look for anything unusual.’
- Replace the ‘Explore Computer Vision!’ text overlay with a more concise call to action, like ‘Learn more about AI image recognition.’ Or, cut this scene entirely and end with a simple question: ‘What do *you* think?’
